# Kettlebird Tea voice guide

## The feeling

Reading a Kettlebird email should feel like the first sip from a fresh pot: warm, quiet, and worth slowing down for. We write like a friend who knows a lot about tea and never shows off about it.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Warm | Gushing or sugary |
| Sensory | Vague ("amazing flavor") |
| Unhurried | Pushy, urgent, salesy |
| Knowledgeable | Snobbish or full of jargon |
| Honest | Hypey or overpromising |

## Tone in practice

### Subject lines

| ✅ Good | ❌ Bad | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Three teas, one quiet morning a month | 🔥 UNLOCK Your BEST Tea Ever!!! | Calm and specific, versus caps, hype and a banned word |
| Your first cup is waiting | Don't miss out — 24 hours left | An invitation, versus pressure |
| What's in the kettle this autumn | Newsletter #12 | Has a reason to open, versus nothing |

### Openings

✅ *"There's a moment, just after you pour, when the steam carries the whole tea up to meet you."*
❌ *"We're SO excited to announce our revolutionary new product that will elevate your tea game!"*

### Describing tea

✅ *"A bright, honeyed Darjeeling with a finish like ripe apricot."*
❌ *"An amazing, delicious tea with incredible flavor."*

### Calls to action

✅ "Join The Steep Club" · "Find your first tea" · "Read the brewing guide" · "See what's new"
❌ "BUY NOW" · "Click here" · "Act now" · "Shop the sale before it's gone"

### Sign-offs

Signed by a real person or by the team, with a short warm line before the name:
✅ *"Steep slowly, — Mara & the Kettlebird team"*
✅ *"Until the next pot, — The Kettlebird team"*
❌ *"Best regards, Marketing Department"*

## Sensory tea vocabulary

Use these words. Pick the ones that are actually true for the tea.

- **Aroma:** floral, toasty, malty, smoky, grassy, orchid, jasmine, stone fruit, honeyed, woodsy, petrichor
- **Taste:** bright, brisk, mellow, nutty, buttery, vegetal, caramel, cocoa, citrus peel, apricot, muscatel
- **Texture / body:** silky, rounded, light-bodied, full-bodied, brothy, crisp, lingering
- **Liquor color:** pale gold, amber, copper, jade, deep ruby
- **Ritual:** steep, pour, unfurl, the first sip, kettle song, a second infusion, a quiet minute

## Banned words and phrases

These never appear in Kettlebird copy. Check case-insensitively, including other forms (unlocks, elevated, leveraging).

- delve
- unlock
- game-changer / game changer
- elevate
- revolutionary
- synergy
- seamless
- leverage
- supercharge
- next-level
- must-have
- act now
- limited time only
- don't miss out
- best ever
- "guilt-free"
- "detox" (we make no health claims)

Also avoid: health or medical claims of any kind, ALL CAPS words, more than one exclamation mark per email, and fake urgency (countdowns, "only 3 left" unless it's true and in `products.md`).

## Formatting rules

- **Subject:** ≤ 50 characters, sentence case, at most one emoji (prefer none).
- **Preview text:** 40–90 characters, adds something the subject doesn't say.
- **Greeting:** `Hi {{first_name|there}},`
- **Paragraphs:** 1–3 sentences. Lots of white space.
- **Length:** 120–250 words of body copy for most emails.
- **Lists:** bullets are fine for "what's inside" or brewing steps, at most 5 items.
- **One CTA button** per email. Secondary links are plain text.
- **Numbers:** prices written `$28`, temperatures `85°C / 185°F`, times `3 minutes`.
- **Product names** exactly as written in `brand/products.md`. It's "The Steep Club", with a capital T when it starts the name.
- **Footer** always last: mailing address, why they're receiving it, unsubscribe link, manage preferences link.
