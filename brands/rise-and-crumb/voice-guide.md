# Rise & Crumb voice guide

> Sample brand profile. It shows that the email studio works for more than one brand. Rise & Crumb is a fictional artisan sourdough bakery.

## The feeling

A Rise & Crumb email should feel like walking into the bakery at 8 am: warm air, flour on the counter, a crust that crackles as it cools. We write like the baker who's happy to tell you how the loaf was made, and keeps it short because there's dough to shape.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Warm | Gushing or saccharine |
| Rustic | Polished, glossy or corporate |
| Craft-focused | Vague ("amazing bread") |
| Plain-spoken | Precious, snobby or full of baking jargon |
| Honest | Hypey, pushy or overpromising |

## Tone in practice

### Subject lines

| ✅ Good | ❌ Bad | Why |
|---|---|---|
| This weekend's bake, out of the oven Saturday | 🔥 MAGIC Loaves You NEED!!! | Calm and specific, versus caps, hype and a banned word |
| The rye is back on the rack | Artisanal perfection, only this weekend | Plain and concrete, versus a banned phrase and pressure |
| What we're baking this weekend | Bakery Newsletter #7 | A reason to open, versus nothing |

### Openings

✅ *"The first loaves come out at six. You can hear the crust crackle as it cools on the rack."*
❌ *"Our magical, guilt-free bread is artisanal perfection in every bite!"*

### Describing bread

Talk about the work and what you can see, smell and hear: the long ferment, the crust, the crumb.

✅ *"A dark, blistered crust and an open, chewy crumb, with a gentle tang from a 36-hour ferment."*
❌ *"Incredible, delicious bread you'll love."*

### Calls to action

✅ "Reserve your loaf" · "Pre-order for the weekend" · "See this weekend's bake"
❌ "BUY NOW" · "Click here" · "Grab yours before they're gone"

### Sign-offs

Signed by a real baker or the bakery team, with a short warm line before the name:
✅ *"See you at the counter, — Noor & the Rise & Crumb bakers"*
✅ *"Warm from the oven, — The Rise & Crumb team"*
❌ *"Regards, Rise & Crumb Management"*

## Bakery vocabulary

Use the words that are actually true for the bake.

- **Crust:** crackly, blistered, deep-baked, burnished, chestnut-brown, shattering
- **Crumb:** open, glossy, chewy, tender, custardy, close-textured, springy
- **Flavor:** gentle tang, nutty, toasty, caramelized, malty, earthy, buttery, warm spice
- **Craft:** long ferment, starter, hand-shaped, stone-baked, scored, proofed overnight, laminated
- **Ritual:** the first slice, still warm, cooling on the rack, the smell at the door, the 8 am counter

## Banned words and phrases

These never appear in Rise & Crumb copy. Check case-insensitively, including other forms (magical, indulging). The house list in `brand/voice-guide.md` (delve, unlock, elevate, "act now", ...) applies to every brand as well.

- guilt-free
- artisanal perfection
- magic
- to die for
- sinful
- mouthwatering
- superfood
- "clean eating" (we make no health or diet claims)
- "sells out fast" (no fake scarcity)

Also avoid: health, diet or gluten-free claims (sourdough is not gluten-free), ALL CAPS words, more than one exclamation mark per email, and fake urgency. Batch limits are only stated when `products.md` lists them.

## Formatting rules

- **Subject:** ≤ 50 characters, sentence case, at most one emoji (prefer none).
- **Preview text:** 40–90 characters, adds something the subject doesn't say.
- **Greeting:** `Hi {{first_name|there}},`
- **Paragraphs:** 1–3 sentences. Plenty of white space.
- **Length:** 120–250 words of body copy.
- **Lists:** bullets are fine for "what's on the rack" or pickup details, at most 5 items.
- **One CTA button** per email. Secondary links are plain text.
- **Numbers:** prices `$12`, weights `900 g`, times `8 am–1 pm`, days written out (`Saturday`).
- **Product names** exactly as written in `brands/rise-and-crumb/products.md`.
- **Brand name** is "Rise & Crumb" (ampersand, never "Rise and Crumb" in copy). In HTML write `Rise &amp; Crumb`.
- **Footer** always last: mailing address, why they're receiving it, unsubscribe link, manage preferences link.
