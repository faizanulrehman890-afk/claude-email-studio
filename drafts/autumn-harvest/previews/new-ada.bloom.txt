Hi Ada,

We're so glad you're here. If you're wondering where to begin, any of these
three makes a gentle first cup.

The mornings are cooler now, and the kettle seems to sing a little longer.
It feels like the right time to sit with the three teas we always keep on
the shelf.

Here they are, with how we like to brew them:

- First Light Darjeeling, Darjeeling, India (first flush). Bright and
  floral, with a muscatel finish like ripe apricot. $18 / 50 g. Brew at
  85°C / 185°F for 3 minutes.
- Misty Ridge Sencha, Shizuoka, Japan. Grassy, brothy, a clean sweetness.
  $16 / 50 g. Brew at 70°C / 158°F for 1.5 minutes.
- Copper Hollow Oolong, Nantou, Taiwan. Toasty, buttery, roasted nuts and
  honey. $22 / 50 g. Brew at 90°C / 194°F for 3 minutes, then steep it
  again, three times or more.

If the afternoon turns grey, the oolong is our pick. Each pour comes out a
little different, so it rewards a quiet minute and a second infusion.
