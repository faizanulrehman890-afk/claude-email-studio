Hi Hugo,

Thank you for all the pots of Copper Hollow Oolong you've brewed with us. You're a big part of why we wanted to try something new.

There's a moment, just after you pour, when the steam carries the whole tea up to meet you. We wanted to bring you more of those moments, a little different each season.

So we started The Steep Club. At the start of each month, a box arrives with three seasonal single-origin teas, each with tasting notes and a brewing guide card. It's $28 a month, and you can skip or cancel any month from your account.

The first box, Winter 2026, holds:

  • Snow Bud White, Fujian, China: honeysuckle, fresh hay, a soft sweet finish
  • Smoked Pine Lapsang, Wuyi Mountains, China: campfire, pine resin, dried longan
  • Winter Flush Assam, Assam, India: malt, cocoa, a little caramel

Each comes in a 50 g pouch, about 20 cups, so there's room for a second pot of the one you love most.
