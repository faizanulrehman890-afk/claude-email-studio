We're Kettlebird Tea, a small loose-leaf tea company. We source single-origin teas from small farms and send them straight to you, so each cup tastes of one place.

From us you'll get brewing tips, stories behind the leaf, and word when a new tea arrives. Nothing loud, nothing rushed.

If you're wondering where to begin, we'd pour you a cup of First Light Darjeeling. It's a first flush from Darjeeling, India: bright and floral, with a muscatel finish like ripe apricot.

To brew it:

  • Heat water to 85°C / 185°F
  • Steep for 3 minutes
  • Pour, and take a quiet minute with the first sip

It's $18 for a 50 g pouch. If you'd rather something toasty or grassy, Copper Hollow Oolong and Misty Ridge Sencha are waiting on the same shelf.
